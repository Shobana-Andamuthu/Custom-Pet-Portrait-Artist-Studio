Studio Photography Guide

Reference Photo Guide

Simple smartphone photos work wonderfully when following these 3 tips.

Tip 1
Natural Light

Shoot near a bright window or outdoors. Avoid flash to preserve natural coat highlights.

Tip 2
Eye Level

Shoot at your pet's eye level to capture iris color, whiskers, and natural expression.

Tip 3
Authentic Expression

Capture relaxed, everyday poses. Candid phone photos work best.
